01 — Capability

Three ways to put an instrument on orbit.

The work decides the spacecraft. Sometimes that is a satellite of your own. Sometimes it is a pass on a shared constellation, tasked for the place and the hour you name.

01

Dedicated satellites

A spacecraft specified around one mission. Orbit, sensor, duty cycle, and the ground segment that flies it.

02

Shared capacity

Time on a shared constellation when a dedicated spacecraft is more than the work requires. You task the pass. The product comes back to you.

03

Data and tasking

Ask for a collect over a place and a window. Revisit on the order of hours, not days, when the orbit is chosen for that cadence.

02 — Industries

What the orbit is asked to see.

Each plate is a stylized picture of a product the mission can be built to deliver. They are drawings, not collects.

01 — Defense

A tasking relationship you control.

Dedicated spacecraft and reserved capacity for organizations that need a look, or a link, on their own timeline. Who can task, who can see the product, and how long it is kept are written into the engagement. Ask us what we can support. We do not advertise authorizations we do not hold.

02 — Agriculture

Crop condition, on a repeat cycle.

Fields change faster than a seasonal snapshot. A mission can be built to revisit on the cadence of irrigation, stress, and harvest, and to task the passes that matter that week.

03 — Construction

What changed since the last pass.

Corridors, sites, and civil works, compared across collects. The value is the difference: what is new, what moved, what was not there last time. Cadence follows the schedule of the work.

04 — Weather

The system, from above the system.

Wide looks for organizations that forecast, warn, and route around weather. The orbit and the sensor are chosen for the phenomenon you watch.

05 — Energy

Corridors, plants, and the ground between.

Linear infrastructure is costly to patrol and practical to task from orbit. A standing cadence, plus a way to ask for a look when a span needs one.

06 — Maritime

Sea lanes, and the vessels in them.

Coastal and open-water awareness for people who move goods and people. Shared capacity when you need a look. A dedicated spacecraft when the route is the mission.

05 — Security

Commitments, not badges.

  • Encryption

    Mission data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

  • Access

    Only the people on that mission can task it or read its products.

  • Separation

    One customer's tasking and products are kept apart from another's.

  • Terms, in writing

    Retention, residency, and where the system runs are agreed before a contract, and written into it.

These are commitments. This page is not a certification, an authorization, or an audit report.
